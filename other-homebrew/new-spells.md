<i>Evocation Cantrip</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 120 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Instantaneous</li>
</ul>

<u>Telekinetic Yank</u>

<i>Transmutation cantrip</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 120 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Instantaneous</li>
</ul>

You use an invisible force to attempt to roughly yank someone.

You target a creature that you can see within range, and then you have them make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, that creature receives 1d6 bludgeoning damage, and then they are either pushed 5 feet away from you or pulled 5 feet closer to you (your choice).

If the creature is restrained due to the effect of <i>Telekinesis</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, they have disadvantage on that saving throw.

<b><i>At Higher Levels.</i></b> The damage of this spell increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).

<b><i>Spell Lists.</i></b> Sorcerer, Wizard

<h1>1st Level</h1>

<u>Arcane Smite</u>

<i>1st-Level Evocation</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 bonus action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> Self</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Concentration, up to 1 minute</li>
</ul>

When you cast this spell, you choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

The next time that you hit a creature with a weapon attack before this spell ends, your attack inflicts an extra 2d8 of the chosen damage type.

<b><i>At Higher Levels.</i></b> When you cast this spell with a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the extra damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.

<b><i>Spell Lists.</i></b> Artificer, Paladin, Ranger

<u>Binding Chains</u>

<i>1st-Level Conjuration</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 30 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S, M (A short iron chain)</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Concentration, up to 1 minute</li>
</ul>

You target a creature that you can see within range, and then you have them make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, they become restrained for the duration of this spell. A creature who is restrained by this spell can use their action to make a Strength check or a Dexterity check (their choice) against your spell save DC. On a success, this spell ends for them.

<b><i>At Higher Levels.</i></b> When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1st. Those creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

<b><i>Spell Lists.</i></b> Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

<h1>3rd Level</h1>

<u>Elemental Aura</u>

<i>3rd-Level Evocation</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> Self (30-foot radius)</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Concentration, up to 10 minutes</li>
</ul>

Elemental power radiates from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius. Until this spell ends, that aura is centered on you, and it moves with you.

When you cast this spell, you choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder. And when you cast this spell, you can designate any number of creatures that you can see to be unaffected by it.

When an affected creature enters the area of that aura for the first time on a turn or starts their turn there, they have to make a Dexterity saving throw. That creature receives 2d8 of the chosen damage type on a failed save, and they receive half as much damage on a successful save.

<b><i>Spell Lists.</i></b> Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard

    <li><b>Components:</b> V</li>
