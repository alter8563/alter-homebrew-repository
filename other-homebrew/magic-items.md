<h1>Devil May Cry</h1>

<h4>Rebellion</h4>

<i>Weapon (Longsword), Artifact (Requires Attunement)</i>

A broadsword which was created by a legendary demon knight and used by one of his twin sons.

<b><i>Magic Weapon.</i></b> You have a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls that you make with this magic weapon, and you inflict one extra die of slashing damage when you hit anything with it.

<b><i>Charges.</i></b> This weapon has 30 charges, and it regains 5d6 of its expended charges daily at dawn.

<b><i>Chain Slash.</i></b> You can expend 3 charges of this weapon to perform <i>Combo Strike</i><sup><i>HB</i></sup> with it.

<b><i>Slash Rave.</i></b> You can expend 1 charge of this weapon to perform <i>Pushing Attack</i><sup><i>HB</i></sup> with it.

<b><i>Stinger.</i></b> You can expend 2 charges of this weapon to perform <i>Charging Impact</i><sup><i>HB</i></sup> with it.

<b><i>High Time.</i></b> You can expend 1 charge of this weapon to perform <i>Tripping Attack</i><sup><i>HB</i></sup> with it.

<b><i>Overdrive.</i></b> You can use this weapon to make ranged attacks which have a normal range of 30 feet.

The damage for these ranged attacks is the same as the damage for the melee attacks of this weapon.

<b><i>Dance Macabre.</i></b> You can expend 5 charges of this weapon to perform <i>Ferocious Barrage</i><sup><i>HB</i></sup> with it.

<b><i>Blade Of Unification.</i></b> You can use this weapon to cast <i>Heal</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.

Once this property is used, it cannot be used again until the next dawn.
