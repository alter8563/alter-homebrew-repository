<h1>Cantrips</h1>

<h4>Ray Of Frost</h4>

<b><i>Source.</i></b> Player’s Handbook

<i>Evocation Cantrip</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 120 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Instantaneous</li>
</ul>

A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks out from you.

You make a ranged spell attack on a creature that you can see within range. On a hit, that creature receives 1d8 cold damage, and then they have their speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

<b><i>At Higher Levels.</i></b> The damage of this spell increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

<b>Spell Lists:</b> Artificer, Sorcerer, Wizard

<h4>Find Familiar</h4>

<b><i>Source.</i></b> Player’s Handbook

<i>1st-Level Conjuration</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 30 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S, M (10 gp worth of charcoal, incense, and herbs which must be consumed by fire in a brass brazier)</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Instantaneous</li>
</ul>

You gain the service of a familiar, a spirit which takes an animal form of your choice: bat, cat, crab, frog, hawk, lizard, octopus, owl, poisonous snake, quipper, rat, raven, sea horse, spider, or weasel. Your familiar appears in an unoccupied space within range, and it has the statistics of the chosen form, except it is a celestial, fey, or fiend (your choice) instead of a beast.

Your familiar acts independently of you, but it always obeys your commands. In combat, it rolls its own initiative and acts on its own turn. Your familiar cannot attack, but it can take other actions as normal.

When your familiar drops to 0 hit points, it disappears, leaving behind no physical form. It reappears after you cast this spell again. As an action, you can temporarily dismiss your familiar to a pocket dimension. Alternatively, you can dismiss it forever. As an action while it is temporarily dismissed, you can cause it to reappear in any unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. Whenever your familiar drops to 0 hit points or disappears into that pocket dimension, it leaves behind in its space anything it was wearing or carrying.

While your familiar is within 2 miles of you, you can telepathically communicate with it. Additionally, you can use your action to see what your familiar sees and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of any special senses that the familiar has. During this time, you are deaf and blind with regard to your own senses.

You cannot have more than one familiar at a time. If you cast this spell while you already have a familiar, you instead cause it to adopt a new form. You choose one of the forms from the above list. Your familiar transforms into the chosen creature.

Finally, when you cast a spell with a range of touch, your familiar can deliver the spell as if it had cast the spell. Your familiar must be within 2 miles of you, and it must use its reaction to deliver the spell when you cast it. If the spell requires an attack roll, you use your attack modifier for the roll.

<b>Spell Lists:</b> Wizard

<b><i>Source.</i></b> Xanathar’s Guide To Everything

<i>4th-Level Transmutation</i>

<ul>

    <li><b>Components:</b> V</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Concentration, up to 10 minutes</li>

<ul>

</ul>

<h1>5th Level</h1>

<h4>Telekinesis</h4>

<b><i>Source.</i></b> Player’s Handbook

<i>5th-Level Transmutation</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 1 action</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> 60 feet</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> Concentration, up to 10 minutes</li>
</ul>

You gain the ability to move creatures or objects with just a mere thought.

When you cast this spell or by using your action on a subsequent turn, you can exert your will on one creature or one object that you can see within range, causing the appropriate effect below. You can affect the same target round after round, or you can choose a new target to affect. If you switch targets, the previous target is no longer affected by this spell.

<b></i>Creature.</i></b> You can try to move a creature. You have to make an ability check with your spellcasting ability which is contested by that creature making an ability check with their Strength. If you win that contest, you move that creature up to 30 feet in any direction, including upward, but not beyond the range of this spell. Until the end of your next turn, that creature is restrained in your telekinetic grip. A creature held upward in this way is suspended in midair.

On a subsequent turn, you can use your action to attempt to maintain your telekinetic grip on the same creature by repeating that contest.

<b><i>Object.</i></b> You can try to move an object which weighs up to 1 ton. If that object is not being worn or carried, you automatically move it up to 30 feet in any direction, including upward, but not beyond the range of this spell. An object held upward in this way is suspended in midair.

If that object is being worn or carried by another creature, you have to make an ability check with your spellcasting ability which is contested by that creature making an ability check with their Strength. If you win that contest, you pull that object away from that creature and can move it up to 30 feet in any direction, including upward, but not beyond the range of this spell.

You can exercise fine control on certain objects with your telekinetic grip, such as manipulating a simple tool, opening a door or a container, stowing or retrieving an item from an open container, or pouring the contents from a vial.

<i>6th-Level Abjuration</i>

<ul>
    <li><b>Casting Time:</b> 10 minutes</li>
    <li><b>Range:</b> Touch</li>
    <li><b>Components:</b> V, S, M (a sprinkling of holy water, rare incense, and a powdered ruby worth at least 1,000 gp)</li>
    <li><b>Duration:</b> 1 day</li>

You create a ward against magical travel that protects up to 40,000 square feet of floor space to a height of 30 feet above the floor. For the duration, creatures can’t teleport into the area or use portals, such as those created by the gate spell, to enter the area. The spell proofs the area against planar travel, and therefore prevents creatures from accessing the area by way of the Astral Plane, Ethereal Plane, Feywild, Shadowfell, or the plane shift spell.

In addition, the spell damages types of creatures that you choose when you cast it. Choose one or more of the following: celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead. When you cast this spell, you can designate a password. A creature that speaks the password as it enters the area takes no damage from the spell.

This spell’s area can’t overlap with the area of another forbiddance spell. If you cast forbiddance every day for 30 days in the same location, the spell lasts until it is dispelled, and the material components are consumed on the last casting.
