<h1>Maneuver Rules</h1>

The actions of a creature can only be affected by one maneuver at a time. A number of maneuvers require creatures to make saving throws, and the saving throw DC for those maneuvers is calculated as such:

<b>Maneuver save DC</b> = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier or your Dexterity modifier (your choice)

<h1>1st-Level Maneuvers</h1>

<u>Brace</u>

If another creature that you can see enters the melee range of your unarmed strike or your melee weapon, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make an opportunity attack on that creature.

<u>Deflect</u>

If another creature that you can see inflicts damage to you with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You decrease that damage by a number equal to 1d8 + your Strength modifier or your Dexterity modifier (your choice) + your level.

<u>Disarming Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, you make them drop one object of your choice in their grasp.

<u>Dodge Roll</u>

If another creature that you can see misses you with an attack roll, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You move up to your movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<u>Field Assessment</u>

If you make an initiative roll, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You switch places in the initiative order with a friendly creature that you can see who is also part of that combat.

<u>Parry</u>

If another creature that you can see targets you for an attack roll, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You add 1d8 to your AC for that attack roll.

<u>Protect</u>

If another creature that you can see targets a different creature that you can see within your reach for an attack roll, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You force that attack roll to be made on you instead.

<u>Pushing Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Strength saving throw, provided they are no more than one size larger than you. On a failure, you push them up to 20 feet away from you.

<u>Redirect</u>

If another creature that you can see misses you with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You have that creature reroll their attack roll for a new weapon attack on a different creature that you can see within 5 feet of you.

<u>Riposte</u>

If another creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon misses you with an attack roll, you can spend 1 maneuver point with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make a weapon attack on that creature.

<u>Taunting Prod</u>

You can spend 1 maneuver point with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You target one creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature becomes taunted by you until the start of your next turn.

<u>Tripping Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Strength saving throw, provided they are no more than one size larger than you. On a failure, you knock them prone.

<u>Variable Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point to perform this maneuver. You choose to either inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, inflict an extra 2d4 damage to that creature, or inflict an extra 2d8 damage of one of the following damage types of your choice to that creature: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

<h1>2nd-Level Maneuvers</h1>

<u>Attack Order</u>

You can spend 2 maneuver points with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You target a friendly creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature can then use their reaction to make one weapon attack.

<u>Charging Impact</u>

You can spend 2 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You move up to your movement speed, and then you can make a melee weapon attack on another creature that you can see within the melee range of your unarmed strike or your melee weapon.

<u>Clever Aptitude</u>

If you make an ability check, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You gain advantage on your ability check. But if you already had advantage on your ability check, you roll an additional d20 instead, taking the highest roll between every dice.

<u>Flash Step</u>

You can spend 2 maneuver points with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You teleport yourself to an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet of you.

<u>Fling</u>

While you are grappling another creature, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You have that creature make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, you hurl them up to 60 feet away from you.

<u>Intercept</u>

If another creature that you can see inflicts damage to you with a weapon attack, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You decrease that damage by a number equal to 1d8 + your Strength modifier or your Dexterity modifier (your choice) + your level. And if you decrease that damage to 0 in this way, you can use that same reaction to make a weapon attack on that creature.

<u>Joint Strike</u>

If a friendly creature that you can see hits another creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon with a weapon attack, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make a weapon attack on the attacked creature.

<u>Movement Order</u>

You can spend 2 maneuver points with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You target a friendly creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature can then use their reaction to move up to their movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<u>Revenge Strike</u>

If another creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon hits a friendly creature that you can see with a weapon attack, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make a weapon attack on the attacking creature.

<u>Stumbling Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 1 maneuver point to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Strength saving throw, provided they are no more than one size larger than you. On a failure, you push them up to 20 feet away from you and knock them prone.

<u>Wide Strike</u>

If you miss another creature that you can see with a weapon attack, you can spend 2 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You reroll your attack roll for a new weapon attack on a different creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon.

<h1>3rd-Level Maneuvers</h1>

<u>Combo Strike</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 3 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make another weapon attack on that creature. But if that attack hits, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage roll.

<u>Disorientating Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 3 maneuver points to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they become blinded, deafened, or muted (your choice) until the start of your next turn.

<u>Emergency Getaway</u>

If another creature that you can see hits you with an attack roll, you can spend 3 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You move up to your movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<u>Goading Taunt</u>

You can spend 3 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You target up to five other creatures that you can see within 30 feet of you. Each of those creatures become taunted by you until the start of your next turn.

<u>Precise Finish</u>

If you make a weapon attack, you can spend 3 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You gain advantage on your attack roll. But if you already had advantage on your attack roll, you roll an additional d20 instead, taking the highest roll between every dice.

<u>Rally</u>

You can spend 3 maneuver points with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You target a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. That creature then gains a number of temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Strength modifier or your Dexterity modifier (your choice) + your level.

<u>Retaliate</u>

If another creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon hits you with an attack roll, you can spend 3 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You make a weapon attack on that creature.

<u>Steel Wind Impact</u>

You can spend 3 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You target another creature that you can see within 30 feet of you. You then make a melee weapon attack on that creature as if you were within 5 feet of them. You can then teleport yourself to an unoccupied space that you can see within 5 feet of that creature.

<u>Sturdy Resilience</u>

If you make a saving throw, you can spend 3 maneuver points with your reaction to perform this maneuver. You gain advantage on your saving throw. But if you already had advantage on your saving throw, you roll an additional d20 instead, taking the highest roll between every dice.

<h1>4th-Level Maneuvers</h1>

<u>Charging Assault</u>

You can spend 4 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You move up to your movement speed. And during that movement, you can make a melee weapon attack on any other creature that you can see who enters the melee range of your unarmed strike or your melee weapon. You can only make one melee weapon attack on any given creature during that movement though. But if any of those attacks hit, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage rolls.

<u>Coordinated Attack</u>

You can spend 4 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You target up to five friendly creatures that you can see within 30 feet of you. Each of those creatures can then use their reaction to make one weapon attack.

<u>Coordinated Movement</u>

You can spend 4 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You target up to five friendly creatures that you can see within 30 feet of you. Each of those creatures can then use their reaction to move up to their movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<u>Stunning Attack</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 4 maneuver points to perform this maneuver. You inflict one extra die of damage to that creature, and then you have that creature make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they become stunned until the start of your next turn.

<u>Tornado Volley</u>

You can spend 4 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You select a space that you can see within the range of your ranged weapon. You then use that ranged weapon to make a weapon attack on every other creature within 10 feet of that space. But if any of those attacks hit, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage rolls.

<u>Undying Spirit</u>

If you make a death saving throw, you can spend 4 maneuver points to perform this maneuver. You add 1d8 to your death saving throw. And if the result of your roll is 20 or higher, you regain 1 hit point as if you had rolled a 20.

<u>Whirlwind Assault</u>

You can spend 4 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You make a weapon attack on every other creature around you within the melee range of your unarmed strike or your melee weapon. But if any of those attacks hit, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage rolls.

<h1>5th-Level Maneuvers</h1>

<u>Assemble</u>

You can spend 5 maneuver points with your bonus action to perform this maneuver. You target up to five creatures that you can see within 30 feet of you. Each of those creatures then gain a number of temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Strength modifier or your Dexterity modifier (your choice).

<u>Blaze Of Glory</u>

If you drop to 0 hit points and do not die outright, you can spend 5 maneuver points to perform this maneuver. You delay yourself from falling unconscious. You then move up to your movement speed, and then you can make a weapon attack on another creature that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon. But if you still have 0 hit points afterward, you fall unconscious.

<u>Critical Hit</u>

If you hit another creature with a weapon attack, you can spend 5 maneuver points to perform this maneuver. You turn your normal hit on that creature into a critical hit. But if you had already scored a critical hit on that creature, you quadruple the dice for your damage roll instead of doubling them.

<u>Ferocious Barrage</u>

You can spend 5 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You make up to five weapon attacks in a row on any other creatures that you can see within the range of your unarmed strike or your weapon. But if any of those attacks hit, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage rolls.

<u>Steel Wind Assault</u>

You can spend 5 maneuver points with your action to perform this maneuver. You target up to five other creatures that you can see within 30 feet of you. You then make a melee weapon attack on each of those creatures as if you were within 5 feet of each of them. But if any of those attacks hit, you cannot add your ability modifier to your damage rolls. You can then teleport yourself to an unoccupied space that you can see within 5 feet of one of those creatures.
