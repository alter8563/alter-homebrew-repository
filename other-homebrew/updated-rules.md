<h1>Attachable Cloaks</h1>

A creature can now use their action to attach a cloak in their main hand to a suit of armor in their off hand. A suit of armor and cloak which are attached to each other are counted as one item for the purpose of effects.

An artifact cloak cannot be attached to a suit of armor with a rarity of legendary or lesser.

<h1>Automatic Critical Hits</h1>

Now if a creature hits anything with an attack roll, they automatically score a critical hit if their attack roll exceeds the AC of their target by 10 or more.

<h1>Automatic Success With Expertise</h1>

If you are using the modified rule for automatic success which accounts for proficiency, you can further modify it to account for expertise.

Now if a player character can apply an expertise bonus to an ability check, they automatically succeed if its DC is less than or equal to the relevant ability score + 5.

<h1>Bite Attack For Player Characters</h1>

Any teeth that a player character has are now natural weapons that they can use for their unarmed strikes. When they hit with them, they inflict piercing damage instead of bludgeoning damage.

<h1>Critical Failure On Certain Saving Throws</h1>

The abilities of certain monsters which have additional effects if another creature fails a saving throw by 5 or more are being changed.

The additional effects now occur if another creature fails that saving throw by 10 or more instead of by 5 or more.

Monsters such as the following ones are subject to being changed in this way:

<ul>
   <li>Basilisk</li>
   <li>Ghost</li>
   <li>Mummy</li>
</ul>

<h1>Critical Success On Certain Saving Throws</h1>

Now when a creature is subjected to an effect which has them make a saving throw to receive half damage, they receive no damage if their saving throw exceeds the DC of that effect by 10 or more.

<h1>Damage Resistance Before Damage Reduction</h1>

Damage resistance is now calculated before damage reduction instead of after it.

<h1>Fixed Blowgun Damage</h1>

Blowguns now inflict 1d1 piercing damage instead of 1 piercing damage.

<h1>Fixed Unarmed Strike Damage For Player Characters</h1>

Unarmed strikes for player characters now inflict 1d1 bludgeoning damage instead of 1 bludgeoning damage.

<h1>Improved Grappling For Player Characters</h1>

Player characters can now use a limb that they are grappling another creature with to make unarmed strikes on that creature.

<h1>Improved Saving Throws For Player Characters</h1>

Now when a player character makes a saving throw which uses one of their ability scores, they gain a bonus to that saving throw equal to their proficiency bonus.

<h1>Limited Short Rests</h1>

A creature can now only benefit from up to two short rests before they must finish a long rest to benefit from any more.

<h1>New Conditions</h1>

The following conditions are new additions which can be applied to any creature:

<b>Confused.</b> You cannot use your reaction.

At the start of each of your turns, you roll a d10 to determine your behavior on that turn.

<b>Frozen.</b> You cannot move, and you can only speak falteringly.

<b>Hastened.</b> Your speed is doubled.

You have a +2 bonus to your AC and your Dexterity saving throws.

You have an additional action that you can use on each of their turns. That action can only be used to make one weapon attack or to take the Dash action, the Disengage action, the Dodge action, the Escape action, the Help action, the Hide action, the Search action, or the Throw action.

<b>Muted.</b> You cannot speak, and you automatically fail any ability check which requires speaking.

<b>Slowed.</b> Your speed is halved.

You have a -2 penalty to your AC and your Dexterity saving throws.

You can only use your action or your bonus action on each of your turns, not both of them.

You cannot use your reaction.

<b>Taunted.</b> You have disadvantage on attack rolls made on creatures other than the creature who you are taunted by.

<h1>New Weapons</h1>

The following weapons are new additions which can be used by any creature:

<b>Cestus.</b> This is a martial melee weapon which inflicts 1d6 piercing damage on a hit. It has the light property and the special property, and it has a price of 20 gp. It also has a weight of 4 pounds.

<b><i>Special Property: Cestus.</i></b> You cannot be forcibly made to drop this weapon.

<b>Submachine Gun.</b> This is a modern firearm which inflicts 2d4 piercing damage on a hit. It has the ammunition property (range 60/180), the burst fire property, and the reload property (30 shots). It also has a weight of 4 pounds.

<h1>Proper List Of Complex Tasks</h1>

Complex tasks consist of:

<ul>
    <li>Loading ammunition into a weapon</li>
    <li>Using a magic item</li>
    <li>Using a tool</li>
    <li>Wielding a shield</li>
    <li>Wielding a weapon</li>
</ul>

<h1>Proper List Of Simple Tasks</h1>

Simple tasks consist of:

<ul>
    <li>Climbing a wall or a ceiling</li>
    <li>Grappling a creature</li>
    <li>Holding an item</li>
    <li>Making an unarmed strike</li>
    <li>Opening or closing a door</li>
    <li>Opening or closing a container</li>
    <li>Providing the somatic components for a spell</li>
    <li>Pushing, dragging, or lifting an object</li>
</ul>

<h1>Spell Scrolls For Ineligible Creatures</h1>
Creatures are now capable of reading spell scrolls of any level, regardless of whether or not they are a spellcaster or whether or not that spell is on their spell list.

But if a creature is not a spellcaster or does not have that spell on their spell list, they have to make an ability check to determine whether or not they can successfully cast it.

Their proficiency bonus is applied to that ability check, but their spellcasting ability modifier is treated as +0. The DC for that ability check is 10 + the level of the spell.

<h1>Super Advantage With Inspiration</h1>

Player characters are now capable of using inspiration on rolls that they already have advantage on. If they do so, they roll an additional d20, taking the highest roll between every dice.

<h1>Throw Action For Player Characters</h1>

Player characters now have a new action known as the Throw action.

While a player character is grappling another creature, they can use the Throw action to have that creature make a Strength saving throw. The DC for this saving throw is 8 + their proficiency bonus + their Strength modifier or their Dexterity modifier (their choice). On a failure, they hurl that creature up to 20 feet away from them.

<h1>Variable Damage For Piercing Weapons</h1>

<h1>Variable Damage For Slashing Weapons</h1>
