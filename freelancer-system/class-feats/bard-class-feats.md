<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Tactical Support I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Charisma Of 13 Or Higher, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You now have a pool of d4 support dice which can hold a maximum of two dice.

You can now spend one support die with your bonus action to give it to another creature that you can see within 30 feet of you.

Now if a creature who has one of your support dice makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, they can spend that die and roll it, adding its result to that attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

Other creatures can only keep one support die at a time, and they lose it when they finish a long rest.

You regain all of your spent support dice when you finish a long rest.

<u>Tactical Support II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Tactical Support I</i>

Your support dice turn into d6 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to four dice.

<u>Tactical Support III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Tactical Support II</i>

Your support dice turn into d8 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to six dice.

<u>Tactical Support IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Tactical Support III</i>

Your support dice turn into d12 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to ten dice.
