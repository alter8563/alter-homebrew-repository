<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<h4>Planar Resonance -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

When you get this class feat, you take a Planar Resonance Passive Effect that you qualify for.

<h2>List Of Planar Resonance Passive Effects</h2>

<h4>Air</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

Your jump distance is no longer halved due to you making a standing high jump, and your jump distance is no longer halved due to you making a standing long jump.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Heart Of The Sky].</i>

<h4>Clarity</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Empathetic Mind</i>

You are no longer prevented from telepathically communicating with creatures who do not understand any languages, but you can only do so by exchanging sensory messages with them.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Sympathetic Mind].</i>

<h4>Darkness</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Night Vision</i>

Your darkvision is no longer impeded by magical darkness.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Umbral Vision].</i>

<h4>Earth</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

You can now climb across difficult surfaces and upside down on ceilings, without requiring an ability check.

You can also now move along walls and ceilings, without requiring both of your empty hands.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Heart Of The Land].</i>

<h4>Insanity</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Empathetic Mind</i>

Your thoughts can no longer be read unless you allow them to be.

And now if another creature attempts to read your thoughts, you can have them make an Intelligence saving throw. The saving throw DC for this effect is equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier, your Wisdom modifier, or your Charisma modifier (your choice when you get this Planar Resonance Passive Effect).

On a failure, that creature gains a long-term madness effect.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Antipathetic Mind].</i>

<h4>Life</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

Now when you use an effect to make a creature regain hit points, you increase the number of hit points that they regain by a number equal to your Constitution modifier.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Marvelous Healing].</i>

<h4>Protection</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

When you get this Planar Resonance Passive Effect, you choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

You now have resistance to the chosen damage type.

And if the damage type that you had chosen was poison, you also now have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Elemental Resistance].</i>

<h4>Time</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Night Vision</i>

You now add your Constitution modifier to your initiative rolls.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Chronal Vision].</i>

<h4>Water</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

You can now breathe underwater.

You also now have immunity to damage from water pressure.

<i>Restrictions: If you are using the Custom Race option, you must not have [Heart Of The Sea].</i>
