<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<h4>Favored Prey I -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Wisdom Of 13 Or Higher, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

When you get this class feat, you choose one of the following creature types: aberration, beast, celestial, construct, dragon, elemental, fey, fiend, giant, humanoid, monstrosity, ooze, plant, or undead.

The chosen creature type is now your Favored Prey.

You can now add 1d4 to attack rolls made on your Favored Prey and to saving throws made against their spells and other abilities.

<h4>Favored Prey II -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Favored Prey I</i>

You can now add 1d6 instead of 1d4 to attack rolls made on your Favored Prey and to saving throws made against their spells and other abilities.

<h4>Favored Prey III -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Favored Prey II</i>

You can now add 1d8 instead of 1d6 to attack rolls made on your Favored Prey and to saving throws made against their spells and other abilities.

<h4>Favored Prey IV -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Favored Prey III</i>

You can now add 1d12 instead of 1d8 to attack rolls made on your Favored Prey and to saving throws made against their spells and other abilities.
