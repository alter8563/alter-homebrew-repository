<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Prodigious Intuition I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Intelligence Of 13 Or Higher, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You now have a pool of d4 intuition dice which can hold a maximum of two dice.

Now if you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can spend one of your intuition dice and roll it, adding its result to that attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

You regain all of your spent intuition dice when you finish a long rest.

<u>Prodigious Intuition II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Prodigious Intuition I</i>

Your intuition dice turn into d6 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to four dice.

<u>Prodigious Intuition III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Prodigious Intuition II</i>

Your intuition dice turn into d8 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to six dice.

<u>Prodigious Intuition IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Prodigious Intuition III</i>

Your intuition dice turn into d12 dice, and the maximum for your pool of them is increased to ten dice.
