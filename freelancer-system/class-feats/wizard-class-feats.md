<h4>Limited Spellcasting I -GCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Spellcasting I, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You now have one 2nd level spell slot, and you learn one new spell that you have spell slots for from your freelancer spell list.

You also now have a pool of maneuver points which can hold a maximum of 4 points. You are also now capable of performing 1st level maneuvers, and you learn three maneuvers that you are capable of performing.

You regain all of your spent maneuver points when you finish a short rest or a long rest.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace a maneuver that you know with a different maneuver that you are capable of performing.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Spellcasting II].</i>

<h4>Magical Discipline -GCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Spellcasting V</i>

When you get this class feat, you choose a 6th level spell, 7th level spell, 8th level spell, and 9th level spell from your freelancer spell list.

You can now use this feature to cast all of the chosen spells, but you can only do so once for each of them before you must finish a long rest to do so again.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace one of the chosen spells with a different spell of the same level from your freelancer spell list.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Spellcasting VI].</i>

<h4>Magical Research I -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Intelligence Of 13 Or Higher, Spellcasting I/Pact Spellcasting I</i>

When you get this class feat, you choose two spells from any spell list. You must choose spells of 1st level or higher that you have spell slots for. Those spells become part of an expanded spell list for you.

You then prepare up to half of the spells from your expanded spell list. You can now cast prepared spells in addition to spells that you already know.

Now if you finish a long rest, you can replace any number of prepared spells with any number of unprepared spells from your expanded spell list.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace the spells in your expanded spell list with different spells from any spell list. You must still choose spells of 1st level or higher that you have spell slots for.

<h4>Magical Research II -LCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Magical Research I</i>

When you get this class feat, you choose two spells from any spell list, provided they are not already part of your expanded spell list. You must choose spells of 1st level or higher that you have spell slots for.

Those spells become part of your expanded spell list. You then prepare up to half of the spells from your expanded spell list.

<h4>Magical Research III -LCF-</h4>

<h4>Spellcasting I -GCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Arcane Initiate</i>

When you get this class feat, you choose one of the following spell lists: bard (along with sorcerer), cleric (along with paladin), druid (along with ranger), or wizard (along with artificer).

The spells from the chosen spell list become part of a freelancer spell list for you.

You now have three 1st level spell slots, and you learn three spells that you have spell slots for from your freelancer spell list.

You regain all of your expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace a spell that you know with a different spell that you have spell slots for from your freelancer spell list.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Pact Spellcasting I].</i>
