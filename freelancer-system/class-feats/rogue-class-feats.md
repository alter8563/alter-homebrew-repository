<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Sneak Attack I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Dexterity Of 13 Or Higher, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You now have Sneak Attack damage of 2d4.

Now if you hit another creature with a weapon attack which uses Dexterity, you can inflict extra damage equal to your Sneak Attack damage, provided you had advantage on your attack roll.

<u>Sneak Attack II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Sneak Attack I</i>

Your Sneak Attack damage is increased to 4d4.

<u>Sneak Attack III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Sneak Attack II</i>

Your Sneak Attack damage is increased to 6d4.

<u>Sneak Attack IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Sneak Attack III</i>

Your Sneak Attack damage is increased to 10d4.
