<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Wild Shape I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Wisdom Of 13 Or Higher, Spellcasting I/Pact Spellcasting I</i>

You can now use your action to enter your Wild Shape, polymorphing yourself into a beast which has a challenge rating of ½ or lower and does not have a swimming speed or flying speed.

Now while your Wild Shape is active, the following rules apply to you:

<ul>
    <li>Your game statistics are replaced by those of the chosen beast, but you retain your alignment and your Intelligence score, Wisdom score, and Charisma score.</li>
    <li>You assume the hit points of the chosen beast. And when you revert to your normal form, you return to the number of hit points that you had before you polymorphed yourself. But if you revert as a result of dropping to 0 hit points, any excess damage carries over to your normal form. As long as that excess damage does not reduce your normal form to 0 hit points, you are not knocked unconscious.</li>
    <li>You are limited in the actions that you can perform by the nature of your beast form. You also cannot speak, and you cannot cast spells or concentrate on them.</li>
    <li>Your equipment melds into your beast form. You cannot activate, wield, or otherwise benefit from any of that equipment.</li>
</ul>

Your Wild Shape lasts for 1 hour or until you end it yourself, drop to 0 hit points, get your form polymorphed, fall unconscious, or die.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<u>Wild Shape II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Wild Shape I</i>

You can now use your Wild Shape to transform into beasts which have a challenge rating of 1 or lower.

<u>Wild Shape III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Wild Shape II</i>

You can now use your Wild Shape to transform into beasts which have a challenge rating of 2 or lower.

<u>Wild Shape IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Wild Shape III</i>

You can now use your Wild Shape to transform into beasts which have a challenge rating of 4 or lower.
