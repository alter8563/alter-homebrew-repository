<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Magical Aura I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Charisma Of 13 Or Higher, Spellcasting I/Pact Spellcasting I</i>

You now have a Magical Aura which has a range of 10 feet around you.

Now while you are not incapacitated, you and friendly creatures in your Magical Aura gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.

<u>Magical Aura II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Magical Aura I</i>

Now while you are not incapacitated, you and friendly creatures in your Magical Aura gain a +2 bonus instead of a +1 bonus to saving throws.

<u>Magical Aura III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Magical Aura II</i>

Now while you are not incapacitated, you and friendly creatures in your Magical Aura gain a +3 bonus instead of a +2 bonus to saving throws.

<u>Magical Aura IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Magical Aura III</i>

Now while you are not incapacitated, you and friendly creatures in your Magical Aura gain a +5 bonus instead of a +3 bonus to saving throws.
