<h4>Limited Pact Spellcasting I -GCF-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Pact Spellcasting I, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

Your spell slots are turned into 2nd level spell slots. You also learn one new spell that you have spell slots for from your freelancer spell list.

You also now have a pool of maneuver points which can hold a maximum of 4 points. You are also now capable of performing 1st level maneuvers, and you learn three maneuvers that you are capable of performing.

You regain all of your spent maneuver points when you finish a short rest or a long rest.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace a maneuver that you know with a different maneuver that you are capable of performing.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Pact Spellcasting II].</i>

<h1>List Of Occult Invocations</h1>

<h4>Agonizing Cantrip</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

You now add your spellcasting ability modifier to the damage of any cantrip that you cast which inflicts damage.

<h4>Battle Cultist</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

You can now use your bonus action to make one weapon attack or take the Dodge action.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<h4>Battle Sorcery</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

Now if you would cast a spell which has a casting time of one action, you could cast it with your bonus action instead.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<h4>Elemental Blast</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: None</i>

When you get this Occult Invocation, you may choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

You can now use this feature to cast <i>Eldritch Blast</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.

And if you had chosen a damage type, the damage type of <i>Eldritch Blast</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup> is now changed to the chosen damage type when you use this feature to cast it.
