<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Magic Item Savant I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You can now attune yourself to up to four magic items instead of up to three magic items.

<u>Magic Item Savant II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Magic Item Savant I</i>

You can now attune yourself to up to five magic items instead of up to four magic items.

<u>Magic Item Savant III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Magic Item Savant II</i>

You can now attune yourself to up to six magic items instead of up to five magic items.

<u>Magic Item Savant IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Magic Item Savant III</i>

You can now attune yourself to up to eight magic items instead of up to six magic items.
