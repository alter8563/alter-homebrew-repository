<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Acrobat -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Acrobatics</i>

You can now use your action or your bonus action on your turn to let yourself ignore difficult terrain until the end of that turn.

<u>Alert -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Survival</i>

Now while you are traveling for one hour or more, you gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>You and up to five other creatures who are traveling with you remain alert to danger while any of you are engaged in other activities such as foraging, navigating, or tracking.</li>
</ul>

<u>Animal Companion -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Animal Handling</i>

You can now spend 8 hours bonding with a friendly beast to make it into your pet. You can only bond with friendly beasts which have a CR of ½ or lower, and you can only have one pet at a time.

You and your pet now gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>The hit point maximum of your pet is increased by a number of points equal to double your level.</li>
    <li>If you take the Attack action on your turn, you can forgo one of your weapon attacks to have your pet make a weapon attack.</li>
    <li>Your pet has a bonus to its AC, saving throw DCs, and damage equal to your proficiency bonus.</li>
    <li>Your pet has a bonus to its attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws equal to your proficiency bonus.</li>
    <li>The weapon attacks of your pet are treated as magical.</li>
</ul>

These benefits last until your pet is replaced by a different pet or dies.

<u>Arcane Sense -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Arcana</i>

You are now permanently under the effects of <i>Detect Magic</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.

<u>Athlete -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Athletics</i>

You now have advantage on saving throws against being forcibly moved or knocked prone.

<u>Beast Speech -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Animal Handling</i>

You can now communicate with beasts as if you shared a language with them.

<u>Blindsight -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Perception</i>

You now have blindsight with a range of 10 feet.

<u>Brave -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Intimidation</i>

You now have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

<u>Cautious -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Insight</i>

You now add your Wisdom modifier to your initiative rolls.

<u>Charming Presence -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Persuasion</i>

You can now use your action to have another creature that you can see within 30 feet of you make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier). On a failure, that creature is charmed by you until the start of your next turn.

<u>Compassionate -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Insight</i>

Now if you interact with another creature outside of combat for at least 10 minutes, you can ascertain what their current emotional state is.

<u>Dancer -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Performance</i>

You can now use the Help action to make a friendly creature within 5 feet of you have advantage on their next attack roll made before the start of your next turn.

<u>Deft Hands -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Sleight Of Hand</i>

You now have two free object interactions that you can use on your turn instead of one.

<u>Detective -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Investigation</i>

Now if you interact with another creature outside of combat for at least 10 minutes, you can learn certain information about their capabilities compared to your own capabilities.

The DM tells you if that creature is equal, inferior, or superior to you in regard to all of the following characteristics:

<ul>
    <li>Strength score</li>
    <li>Dexterity score</li>
    <li>Constitution score</li>
</ul>

<u>Discerning -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Insight</i>

You now have advantage on opportunity attacks made on other creatures.

<u>Divine Sense -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Religion</i>

You are now permanently under the effects of <i>Detect Evil And Good</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.

<u>Elusive -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Deception</i>

Other creatures now have disadvantage on opportunity attacks made on you.

<u>Exorcist -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Religion</i>

You can now sense the presence and location of other creatures who are possessed within 30 feet of you.

This effect can penetrate most barriers, but it is blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 feet of wood or dirt.

<u>Explorer -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Nature</i>

You no longer have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks which rely on sight due to you being in an area of heavy rain or heavy snowfall, and you no longer have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks which rely on hearing due to you being in an area of heavy rain.

<u>Forager -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Survival</i>

Now when you forage, you find twice as much food as normal.

<u>Frightening Presence -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Intimidation</i>

You can now use your action to have another creature that you can see within 30 feet of you make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier). On a failure, that creature is frightened by you until the start of your next turn.

<u>Greedy Plunder -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Sleight Of Hand</i>

Now if an object that you can see falls onto a surface that you can see within your reach, you can use your reaction to pick it up.

<u>Improved Animal Companion -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Expertise With Animal Handling, Animal Companion</i>

<ul>

    <li>Your pet has a bonus to its AC equal to half your proficiency bonus + 1.</li>
    <li>Your pet has a +3 bonus to its damage.</li>
</ul>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Expertise With Arcana, Rapid Identification</i>

Now if you successfully identify a spell that another creature is casting which has you make a saving throw, you gain advantage on your first saving throw against that spell.

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Performance</i>

You can now use your action to start a special performance which distracts other creatures.

When you begin this performance, you have any number of creatures who can see you or hear you make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier).

On a failure, a creature is enraptured by you until they can no longer see you or hear you, they receive damage, or this performance ends.

An enraptured creature has disadvantage on their Intelligence (Investigation) checks and Wisdom (Perception) checks.

This performance lasts until you end it yourself, receive damage, fall unconscious, or die.

This performance also ends early if you do not use your action on each of your subsequent turns to keep it going.
