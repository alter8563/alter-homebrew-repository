<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Alchemist -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Alchemist’s Supplies</i>

You gain expertise with alchemist’s supplies.

And now when you spend money to collect raw materials, you collect 1 additional pound worth of raw materials for every 50 gp spent.

<u>Botanist -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Herbalism Kit</i>

You gain expertise with the herbalism kit.

And now when you use a herbalism kit to craft potions of healing, it takes half as much time compared to normal, and it costs half as much money compared to normal.

<u>Brewer -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Brewer’s Supplies</i>

You gain expertise with brewer’s supplies.

You can also now use brewer’s supplies to purify twice as much water compared to normal.

<u>Burglar -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Thieves’ Tools</i>

You gain expertise with thieves’ tools.

You can also now use your bonus action to use thieves’ tools.

<u>Calligrapher -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Calligrapher’s Supplies</i>

You gain expertise with calligrapher’s supplies.

You also now have advantage on ability checks made to decipher any cipher.

<u>Carpenter -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Carpenter’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with carpenter’s tools.

And now when you fortify a door or a window, you increase the DC needed to open it by 10 instead of 5.

<u>Cartographer -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Cartographer’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with cartographer’s tools.

And now if you have an accurate map while you are traveling for one hour or more, you gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>You and up to five other creatures who are traveling with you cannot become lost by means other than magical ones.</li>
</ul>

<u>Cobbler -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Cobbler’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with cobbler’s tools.

Any creatures who wear shoes that you worked on can also now travel up to 16 hours a day instead of 10 hours a day without making saving throws to avoid exhaustion.

<u>Cook -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Cook’s Utensils</i>

You gain expertise with cook’s utensils.

And now when you use cook’s utensils during a short rest, you increase the number of hit points that you and your companions regain per hit die spent by a number equal to your proficiency bonus.

<u>Counterfeiter -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Forgery Kit</i>

You gain expertise with the forgery kit.

You can also now use a forgery kit to produce forged documents which are twice as long as normal.

<u>Gamer -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With One Type Of Gaming Set</i>

You gain expertise with one type of gaming set that you are proficient with.

<u>Glassblower -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Glassblower’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with glassblower’s tools.

Any damage that you inflict to glass objects is also now always doubled, without requiring you to study them for 1 minute beforehand.

<u>Imposter -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Disguise Kit</i>

You gain expertise with the disguise kit.

And now if you spend 1 hour observing a creature, you can use a disguise kit during a long rest to create a disguise which allows a creature to mimic that creature.

You can also now use your action to don or doff a disguise.

<u>Jeweler -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Jeweler’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with jeweler’s tools.

You can also now use jeweler’s tools during a long rest to cut and polish one uncut gem.

<u>Leatherworker -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Leatherworker’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with leatherworker’s tools.

And now when determining how many workweeks it would take for you to create leather armor or boots by using leatherworker’s tools, you divide the total gold piece cost by 100 instead of 50.

<u>Mason -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Mason’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with mason’s tools.

And now when you are building a stronghold, it takes half as much time compared to normal, and it costs half as much money compared to normal.

<u>Musician -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With One Type Of Musical Instrument</i>

You gain expertise with one type of musical instrument that you are proficient with.

<u>Navigator -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Navigator’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with navigator’s tools.

And now if you can see the sun or the stars while you are traveling for one hour or more, you gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>You and up to five other creatures who are traveling with you cannot become lost by means other than magical ones.</li>
</ul>

<u>Painter -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Painter’s Supplies</i>

You gain expertise with painter’s supplies.

You can also now use painter’s supplies during a long rest to produce a complex work of art.

<u>Poisoner -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Poisoner’s Kit</i>

You gain expertise with the poisoner’s kit.

The saving throw DC of any poison that you create is also now increased by a number equal to your proficiency bonus.

<u>Potter -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Potter’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with potter’s tools.

Any damage that you inflict to ceramic objects is also now doubled.

<u>Rider -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With One Type Of Vehicle</i>

You gain expertise with one type of vehicle that you are proficient with.

<u>Smith -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Smith’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with smith’s tools.

And now when determining how many workweeks it would take for you to create armor or weapons by using smith’s tools, you divide the total gold piece cost by 100 instead of 50.

<u>Tinker -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Tinker’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with tinker’s tools.

And now when you repair a damaged object, you restore an additional 10 hit points to it for every hour of work.

<u>Weaver -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Weaver’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with weaver’s tools.

And now when determining how many workweeks it would take for you to create cloaks or robes by using weaver’s tools, you divide the total gold piece cost by 100 instead of 50.

<u>Woodcarver -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With Woodcarver’s Tools</i>

You gain expertise with woodcarver’s tools.

You can also now use woodcarver’s tools to craft twice as many arrows compared to normal.
