<h1>List Of Class Feats</h1>

<u>Primal Rage I -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Strength Of 13 Or Higher, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You can now use your bonus action to enter your Primal Rage, and you now have a Rage Damage bonus of +2.

Now while your Primal Rage is active, you gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>If you hit with a weapon attack which uses Strength, you have a bonus to your damage roll equal to your Rage Damage bonus.</li>
    <li>You have resistance to bludgeoning damage, piercing damage, and slashing damage.</li>
</ul>

Your Primal Rage lasts for 1 minute or until you end it yourself, fall unconscious, or die.

Your Primal Rage also ends early at the end of your turn if you did not inflict damage or receive damage since the end of your last turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<u>Primal Rage II -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 5th Level, Primal Rage I</i>

Your Rage Damage bonus is increased to +4.

<u>Primal Rage III -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 9th Level, Primal Rage II</i>

Your Rage Damage bonus is increased to +6.

<u>Primal Rage IV -LCF-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: 22nd Level, Primal Rage III</i>

Your Rage Damage bonus is increased to +10.
