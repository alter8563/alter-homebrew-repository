<h2>Creating a Legendary Freelancer</h2>

You should consider what your freelancer requires first and foremost before you adapt to whatever you come across on your adventures. This is less of a proper class and more of a choice list which combines the optional rules of multiclassing and feats. It allows for more flexible customization of characters who utilize the abilities of the base classes in conjunction with one another.

<h3>Quick Build</h3>

You can make a freelancer quickly by following these suggestions. First, set your highest ability score into Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma, followed by Constitution. Second, pick any background. Then you can take class feats which correspond to your chosen ability scores, like Varied Armor Training or Unarmored Defense for defense and Simple Weapon Training, Monastic Arts I, or Arcane Initiate for offense.

<h1>Legendary Class Features</h1>

As a legendary freelancer, you gain the following class features.

<h3>Hit Points</h3>

<b>Hit Dice:</b> 2d8 per freelancer level

<b>Hit Points at 1st Level:</b> 16 + double your Constitution modifier

<b>Hit Points at Higher Levels:</b> 2d8 (or 9) + double your Constitution modifier per freelancer level after 1st

<h3>Proficiencies</h3>

<b>Armor:</b> Light armor and medium armor, or shields, or get [Unarmored Defense] instead

<b>Weapons:</b> Simple weapons, or firearms, or get [Monastic Arts I] or [Arcane Initiate] instead

<b>Tools:</b> None

<b>Saving Throws:</b> Strength, Intelligence, or Charisma and Dexterity, Constitution, or Wisdom

<b>Skills:</b> Choose any two

<h3>Equipment</h3>

You start with 100 gp to buy your own equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background.

<h2>GCF (Greater Class Feat)</h2>

You take a greater class feat that you qualify for. You can only take each greater class feat once.

<h2>LCF (Lesser Class Feat)</h2>

You take a lesser class feat that you qualify for. You can only take each lesser class feat once.

<b>Note: If you would take a normal feat that you qualify for, you must take a lesser class feat that you qualify for instead.</b>

<h2>Ability Score Improvement</h2>

You increase one ability score by 2 or two different ability scores by 1, up to a maximum of 20.

<b>Note: You cannot forgo taking an ability score improvement to take a normal feat that you qualify for instead.</b>

<h1>Legendary Traits</h1>

As a legendary freelancer, you gain the following legendary traits.

<h2>Legendary Actions</h2>

You are now capable of using legendary actions.

You can only use one legendary action at a time, and you can only do so at the end of another creature’s turn.

You have a number of legendary actions that you qualify for equal to half your proficiency bonus + 1.

And now if you gain a level in this class, you can replace a legendary action that you have with a different legendary action that you qualify for.

<h2>Legendary Resistance</h2>

Now if you fail a saving throw, you can make yourself succeed that saving throw instead.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to half your proficiency bonus + 1, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<h1>List Of Legendary Actions</h1>

<h4>Cast A Cantrip</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Arcane Initiate</i>

You cast a cantrip which has a casting time of 1 action or 1 bonus action.

<h4>Cast A Spell (Costs 2 Actions)</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Spellcasting I/Pact Spellcasting I</i>

You cast a spell of 1st level or higher which has a casting time of 1 action or 1 bonus action.

<h4>Dodge</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You take the Dodge action.

<h4>Escape</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You take the Escape action.

<h4>Hide</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You take the Hide action.

<h4>Make A Weapon Attack</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Proficiency With One Type Of Weapon/Monastic Arts I</i>

You make a weapon attack.

<h4>Move</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You move up to your movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<h4>Perform A Maneuver (Costs 2 Action)</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level, Combat Maneuver I/Limited Spellcasting I/Limited Pact Spellcasting I</i>

You perform a maneuver which requires your action or your bonus action.

<h4>Search</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You take the Search action.

<h4>Sprint (Costs 2 Actions)</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You move up to double your movement speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.

<h4>Throw</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You take the Throw action.

<h4>Wrestle</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: 1st Level</i>

You make a grapple attempt or a shove attempt on another creature.

<h1>Variant Playstyle</h1>

If you are playing in a game which involves more than one player character and each of those player characters is using the Legendary Freelancer Class, you forgo having access to Legendary Traits.
