<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<h4>Photosynthesis -3 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Plant)</i>

You now have leaves on your body. If you are exposed to sunlight for 2 hours while you possess those leaves, you are treated as if you consumed one gallon of water. And if you gain nourishment in this way, you also gain the benefits of <i>Lesser Restoration</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.
