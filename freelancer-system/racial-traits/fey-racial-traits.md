<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<u>Fairy Nature -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Fey), Trance</i>

You no longer require food or water to survive, and you no longer require air to breathe. You also now have immunity to disease.

You can also now gain the benefits of a long rest by performing light activities over the course of 8 hours.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>
