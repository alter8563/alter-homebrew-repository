<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<h4>Determination -6 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Humanoid)</i>

Now if you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can make yourself gain advantage on that attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

You can only use this feature one time, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Draconic Breath], [Huge Form], or [Keen Senses].</i>
