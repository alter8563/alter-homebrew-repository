<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<h4>Hazy Memories -2 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Undead)</i>

Now if you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can add 1d4 to that attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

You can only use this feature one time, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Fangs].</i>

<h4>Turn Immunity -2 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Undead)</i>

You now have immunity to effects which turn undead.
