<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<h4>Immutable Form -2 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Construct)</i>

You now have immunity to being petrified.

<h4>Integrated Armor -2 Points-</h4>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Construct)</i>

You can now only don suits of armor that you are proficient with, and you must do so by incorporating them into your body. This process takes 1 hour to complete, and it can be finished during a short rest or long rest. You must also use this process to doff a suit of armor on your body.

Now while you are conscious, a suit of armor that you have donned in this way cannot be removed from your body in any other way.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Natural Armor].</i>
