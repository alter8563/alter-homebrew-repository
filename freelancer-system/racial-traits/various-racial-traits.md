<h1>List Of Racial Traits</h1>

<u>Additional Feat -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Humanoid), Feat</i>

You take a normal feat that you qualify for.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>

<u>Amorphous Form -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Elemental/Ooze)</i>

Now while you are not holding, wearing, or carrying anything, you can move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [False Appearance].</i>

<u>Change Shape -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Construct/Elemental/Humanoid/Ooze/Undead)</i>

You can now use your action to polymorph yourself into a human form with an appearance of your choice. Your statistics and equipment remain the same in your human form.

Your human form lasts until you return to your normal form, use this feature to change the appearance of your human form, or die.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Human Disguise].</i>

<u>Claws -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

You now have claws on your hands and feet. They are natural weapons that you can use for your unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, you inflict piercing damage or slashing damage instead of bludgeoning damage.

<u>Climate Adaptation -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Celestial/Fiend/Humanoid)</i>

You are now naturally adapted to hot climates and cold climates.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Perpetual Order], [Trance], or [Void Breath].</i>

<u>Deeply Unusual Nature -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Construct/Undead), Unusual Nature</i>

You no longer gain exhaustion due to you being in a forced march.

You can also now gain the benefits of a long rest by performing light activities over the course of 8 hours.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>

<u>Draconic Breath -6 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Dragon/Humanoid)</i>

When you get this racial trait, you choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.

You can now use your action to exhale magical energy in a 20-foot cone. Every creature in that area has to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier). A creature receives 1d8 damage of the chosen damage type on a failed save, and they receive half as much damage on a successful save.

This damage is increased to 2d8 at 5th level, 3d8 at 9th level, 4d8 at 13th level, 5d8 at 17th level, 6d8 at 22nd level, 7d8 at 26th level, and 8d8 at 30th level.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Determination], [Huge Form], or [Keen Senses].</i>

<u>Elemental Nature -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Dragon/Elemental/Giant/Ooze/Plant)</i>

You no longer require food or water to survive, and you no longer require air to breathe. You also now have immunity to disease.

You can also no longer be forcibly put to sleep. And you no longer require sleep, but you must enter a meditative state for 6 hours during a long rest to gain its benefits. You are partially conscious in this state, allowing you to still be aware of your surroundings during it.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>

<u>Equine Body -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Humanoid/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

Your legs are replaced by equine legs. While you possess those legs, you count as one size larger for determining which creatures are capable of being mounted by you and which creatures are capable of mounting you.

You also now count as one size larger for determining your carrying capacity and the weight that you can push, drag, or lift.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Powerful Build] or [Serpentine Body].</i>

<u>Extended Longevity -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Humanoid)</i>

You now have a lifespan of 1000 years instead of 100 years.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Feat].</i>

<u>False Appearance -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Elemental/Plant)</i>

You can now use the Hide action to attempt to hide by pretending to be an object. Now while you are hiding in this way and not moving, other creatures have to make Intelligence (Investigation) checks instead of Wisdom (Perception) checks to realize that you are not an object.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Amorphous Form].</i>

<u>Fangs -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Fey/Humanoid/Monstrosity/Undead)</i>

You now have fangs in your mouth. They are natural weapons that you can use for your unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, you inflict piercing damage instead of bludgeoning damage.

And if you hit another creature who is not a construct or an undead with them, you can make yourself regain a number of hit points equal to the piercing damage that you inflict.

You can only make yourself regain hit points in this way a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a long rest.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Hazy Memories].</i>

<u>Feat -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Humanoid)</i>

You take a normal feat that you qualify for.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Extended Longevity].</i>

<u>Godly Nature -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Fiend/Celestial), Climate Adaptation/Perpetual Order</i>

You no longer require food or water to survive, and you no longer require air to breathe. You also now have immunity to disease.

You can also no longer be forcibly put to sleep. And you no longer require sleep, but you must enter a meditative state for 6 hours during a long rest to gain its benefits. You are partially conscious in this state, allowing you to still be aware of your surroundings during it.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>

<u>Horns -1 Point-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

You now have horns on your head. They are natural weapons that you can use for your unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, you inflict piercing damage or slashing damage instead of bludgeoning damage.

<u>Huge Form -6 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Giant/Humanoid)</i>

You can now use your action to increase your size category to huge, provided you are not already huge or larger. Now while you are changed in this way, you gain the following benefits:

<ul>
    <li>Your reach is increased by 10 feet.</li>
    <li>You add 2d4 to damage rolls made for your weapon attacks and your spells.</li>
    <li>You have a -2 penalty to your AC.</li>
</ul>

This change lasts for 10 minutes or until you end it yourself, get your size category decreased, fall unconscious, or die.

You can only use this feature one time, and you regain all of your expended uses when you finish a short rest or a long rest.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Determination], [Draconic Breath], or [Keen Senses].</i>

<u>Human Disguise -4 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Construct/Elemental/Humanoid/Ooze/Undead)</i>

When you get this racial trait, you create a human form with an appearance of your choice. That human form becomes your personal human form.

You can now use your action to polymorph yourself into your personal human form. Your statistics and equipment remain the same in your personal human form.

Your personal human form lasts until you return to your normal form or die.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Change Shape].</i>

<u>Hybrid Feat -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Humanoid), Extended Longevity</i>

You take a normal feat that you qualify for.

<i>Restrictions: You must be using the Legendary Custom Race option.</i>

<u>Keen Senses -6 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

You now have animalistic eyes, an animalistic nose, and animalistic ears. Now while you possess those animalistic body parts, you have advantage on your Wisdom (Perception) checks.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Determination], [Draconic Breath], or [Huge Form].</i>

<u>Natural Armor -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Undead)</i>

Now while you are not wearing a suit of armor, your AC is equal to 12 + your Dexterity modifier or your Constitution modifier (your choice when you get this racial trait).

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Integrated Armor]. And if you are using the Freelancer class, you must not have [Unarmored Defense].</i>

<u>Perpetual Order -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Celestial/Fiend/Humanoid)</i>

Your alignment can no longer be forcibly changed.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Climate Adaptation], [Trance], or [Void Breath].</i>

<u>Robust Shell -4 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Humanoid), Natural Armor</i>

You now have a shell on your upper back. Now while you possess that shell, you have a +1 bonus to your AC, provided you are not wearing a suit of armor.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Wings].</i>

<u>Serpentine Body -3 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Humanoid/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

Your legs are replaced by a lengthy, serpentine tail. It has a reach of 5 feet, and it can be used to perform simple tasks, except for providing somatic components for a spell.

And if you grapple another creature with it, they are restrained until that grapple ends.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Equine Body] or [Tail].</i>

<u>Stinger -1 Point-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead), Tail</i>

You now have a stinger on your tail. It is a natural weapon that you can use for your unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, you inflict piercing damage or slashing damage instead of bludgeoning damage.

<u>Tail -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

You now have a lengthy tail on your lower back. It has a reach of 5 feet, and it can be used to perform simple tasks, except for providing somatic components for a spell.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Serpentine Body].</i>

<u>Trance -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Beast/Fey/Humanoid)</i>

You can no longer be forcibly put to sleep. And you no longer require sleep, but you must enter a meditative state for 6 hours during a long rest to gain its benefits. You are partially conscious in this state, allowing you to still be aware of your surroundings during it.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Climate Adaptation], [Perpetual Order], or [Void Breath].</i>

<u>Unusual Nature -8 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Construct/Undead)</i>

You no longer require food or water to survive, and you no longer require air to breathe. You also now have immunity to disease.

You can also no longer be forcibly put to sleep. And you no longer require sleep, but you must enter a meditative state for 6 hours during a long rest to gain its benefits. You are partially conscious in this state, allowing you to still be aware of your surroundings during it.

<u>Void Breath -2 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Aberration/Beast/Humanoid)</i>

You no longer require air to breathe.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Climate Adaptation], [Perpetual Order], or [Trance].</i>

<u>Wings -6 Points-</u>

<i>Prerequisites: Creature Type (Any Except For Elemental/Ooze/Plant/Undead)</i>

You now have a pair of wings on your upper back. Now if you start moving, you can use those wings to gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed until the end of the current turn. But if you already have a flying speed equal to your walking speed due to something other than your wings, your flying speed is increased by 30 feet instead.

<i>Restrictions: You must not have [Robust Shell].</i>
