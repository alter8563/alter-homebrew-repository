<h1>Option Details</h1>

This homebrew is a variant option in a similar vein to what <i>Tasha’s Cauldron Of Everything</i> introduced with the Custom Lineage option, allowing players to customize the races of their characters. It expands on that concept to allow for more depth with the potential of creating a unique race.

<h1>Custom Race Traits</h1>

All custom races gain the following traits.

<b>Ability Score Increase.</b> You increase two different ability scores by 1.

<b>Creature Type.</b> When you pick this race, you choose one of the following creature types: aberration, beast, celestial, construct, dragon, elemental, fey, fiend, giant, humanoid, monstrosity, ooze, plant, or undead. You are the chosen creature type.

And if the creature type that you had chosen was construct or undead, you can be affected by the following spells which normally have no effect on them: <i>Cure Wounds</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Heal</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Healing Word</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Mass Cure Wounds</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Mass Heal</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Mass Healing Word</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Power Word: Heal</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, <i>Prayer Of Healing</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>, and <i>Spare The Dying</i><sup><i>PHB</i></sup>.

<b>Size.</b> You are medium or small (your choice when you pick this race).

<b>Speed.</b> Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

<b>Lifespan.</b> You have a lifespan of 1000 years. But if your creature type is beast or humanoid, you have a lifespan of 100 years instead.

And if your creature type is construct or undead, you do not have a fixed lifespan, and you cannot have your age be forcibly changed.

<b>Racial Traits.</b> You have 14 points that you must spend to take racial traits that you qualify for. But if your creature type is beast, construct, humanoid, or undead, you have 16 points that you must spend to take racial traits that you qualify for instead. You can only take each racial trait once.

And if your creature type is construct or undead, you must take [Unusual Nature].

<b>Languages.</b> You know Common and one other standard language or exotic language.
